# Bridging Two Essential Tools

Slack and WhatsApp are two of the most widely used communication platforms in Indian businesses. While Slack powers internal team collaboration, WhatsApp connects businesses with customers. Integrating these two creates powerful workflows that can transform how your team operates.

## Why Integrate Slack and WhatsApp?

- **Centralized Notifications**: Get WhatsApp messages in Slack channels
- **Team Collaboration**: Discuss customer queries in real-time
- **Automated Alerts**: Trigger notifications based on WhatsApp events
- **Quick Responses**: Draft and send WhatsApp replies without leaving Slack

## Must-Try Workflows

### 1. Customer Support Alerts

Whenever a high-priority customer message arrives on WhatsApp, automatically post it to a designated Slack channel for immediate attention.

```yaml
Trigger: WhatsApp message with keyword "urgent"
Action: Post to #support-alerts Slack channel
Include: Customer name, message, and quick reply buttons
```

### 2. Team Availability Sync

Update Slack status based on WhatsApp agent availability. When an agent is handling a conversation, their Slack status automatically reflects that they're busy.

### 3. Daily Digest

Send a daily summary of WhatsApp conversations to a Slack channel each morning, highlighting unread messages, response times, and key metrics.

### 4. Escalation Workflow

When a WhatsApp conversation remains unanswered for more than 10 minutes, automatically escalate by posting to a manager's Slack DM and creating a task.

### 5. Lead Distribution

New leads from WhatsApp campaigns automatically get posted to a sales channel in Slack, with buttons to claim the lead.

## Setting Up Your Integration

To implement these workflows, you'll need:

1. A WhatsApp Business API provider with webhook support
2. A Slack workspace with appropriate permissions
3. An automation platform (like Zapier, Make, or custom code)
4. Clear rules and responsibilities

## Conclusion

Slack + WhatsApp integration eliminates silos between internal and external communication. For Indian teams looking to boost productivity and response times, these workflows are essential.
